# ep03 "What If Leveling Up Cost You Years of Your Life?" — script v1 (2026-11-05, ~1:35)

## Premise
Every "level up" makes you ~10 % better at anything and costs one year, taken off the END of your life.

## Facts (checked)
- Big dogs die younger: across 74 breeds, larger breeds age faster; every ~2 kg of body mass costs about 1 month of
  life expectancy (Kraus, Pavard & Promislow 2013, "The size–life span trade-off decomposed: why large dogs die young",
  The American Naturalist 181(4)). Great Dane: ~54–90 kg, typical lifespan 7–10 yrs ("about eight"). Chihuahua: ~1.5–3 kg,
  12–20 yrs ("often makes it to fifteen").
- People value a reward now over the same reward later, steeply at short delays: hyperbolic discounting
  (Ainslie 1975; Laibson 1997). "Why credit cards work": buy now, pay later is the textbook example.
- "A twenty-year-old has sixty years to spend": world life expectancy ~73 (WHO 2019), US ~77 → 55–60 years left at 20.
- Loan gag: borrow 5 years, repay 5.6 = 12 % interest (5 × 1.12).
