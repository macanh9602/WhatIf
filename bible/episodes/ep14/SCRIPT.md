Text: eps/ep14/script.txt. Format: twist-short-script. Voice: Chatterbox (narrator_ref.wav). Status: script + shot list approved by the user 2026-11-07 ("oke làm đi"), ~1:10, olive field jacket.

## Decision (user)
Lead outfit, proposed: the one curl + plain olive field jacket, no cap, no insignia. Default if not changed: red varsity jacket as in the bible.

## Premise
You invade to capture the tunnels. The Swiss defence is to destroy the thing you came for.

## Beats
- Paradox: you invade, and the Swiss blow up their own country.
- Plant: a pink house whose window shines in the shade.
- Mockery: "Their army is bankers and dairy farmers."
- Count up: one village, then two.
- Turn: the house folds open, the bridge goes, the slope comes down.
- You see: the shine was paint, the house was paint, the whole country is wired.
- Button: "You wanted it in one piece. It's in a million." Then the real-life close: "That was the whole point." No loop line.

## Changed from the user's draft, and why
- "From the Cold War": the plan dates from July 1940 and was extended in the Cold War. The script says 1940.
- "Thousands of kg of explosive": no source. Used the sourced "about 3,000 demolition points".
- "Whole mountain front explodes", "houses become bunkers": kept as the invented "you" story, on real mechanisms (fake chalets, fake rocks, rigged slopes).
- Added a motive (the tunnels) so the ending can turn on one phrase, "in one piece".
- Invader unnamed: no real army, flag or uniform.

## True / invented
"You" and the invasion are invented. Switzerland was not invaded in this period. The description must say so.
Draft: "Nobody ever did this: the invasion here is made up. The fake houses, rigged bridges and demolition plan were real."
