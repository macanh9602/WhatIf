## Premise
The smuggler's escape is to destroy his own fortune, because the law makes his hunters rescue him and the evidence is gone. Then the law changes.

## Beats
- Paradox: you sank a hundred million dollars, and your hunters had to save your life.
- Plant: four new life jackets, still in the plastic, on a boat where nothing is new.
- Mockery: "Life jackets? On a submarine?"
- Count up: a plane, then a ship, then a fast boat.
- Turn: a man on the roof, one valve, the sea comes in.
- You see: law of the sea + no cargo = no case. The jackets were the plan.
- Button: 2008 law. "They still have to save your life. Now they can keep fifteen years of it." Picture: a new orange jumpsuit, still in the plastic. No loop line.

## Changed from the user's draft, and why
- "Radar ghost ... underwater at top speed": wrong. These boats are slow (JIATF South: ~11 km/h) and are usually found by patrol aircraft. Hook replaced.
- "US Navy soldiers": boardings are done by Coast Guard teams.
- "40 °C in the cabin": no source. Used sourced fumes, no running water, no sleep.
- "No cargo, no crime": only true before the 2008 law. So the story is set in 2007 and the law becomes the second twist. Keeping the old ending would state something false today.
- Word "cocaine" never spoken or shown (only wrapped bales, "cargo"): advertiser safety, and it is not needed.
- A crime story explains the idea, not a method: no build detail, no valve detail, and the ending says it no longer works.

## True / invented
"You", the crew and this voyage are invented; the pattern is real. The description must say so.
Draft: "The captain in this story is made up. Crews really did sink their boats and get rescued, and since 2008 the US treats the boat itself as a crime."
