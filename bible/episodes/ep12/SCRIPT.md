## Premise
Every time you fall in one world, you wake up falling in the other. Bed -> sky -> bed -> sky, each sky a new place.

## Facts / own calcs
- ~200 km/h: belly-down skydiver terminal velocity ~195 km/h (120 mph), reached in ~12 s (standard skydiving figure;
  NASA Glenn "terminal velocity" page has the formula). Spoken "two hundred kilometres an hour".
- Hypnic jerk: involuntary twitch at sleep onset; prevalence usually given as 60-70 % of people (Sleep Foundation;
  Ohayon 2016 Sleep Medicine survey found ~70 % report sleep starts). Spoken "up to seven in ten". Cause is not settled:
  one theory is the brain reading fast muscle relaxation as falling -> "can mistake it for falling" (hedged on purpose).
- Reality check: lucid-dream research (LaBerge, Lucidity Institute) uses re-reading text / a digital clock; text changes
  on a second look in most dreams. Spoken as "numbers rarely stay put" (no percentage). VERIFY a citable source before the
  description quotes a number.
- Clock times 3:07 / 9:40 are story props, not statistics.

## Chain
roll off bed -> wake falling over town -> twitch just before the roofs -> back in bed, rolling again -> desert, sea,
mountains (each fall real) -> why: muscles relax, brain thinks "falling" -> the twitch (7 in 10 feel it) -> the twitch is the
door -> so read the watch twice -> 3:07 / 9:40 = dream -> don't flinch -> land in a field -> wake on the floor, clock 3:07
twice (answers "never land") -> question.
