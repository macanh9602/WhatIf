## Premise
Sitka, Alaska, 18 Oct 1867: you hold the cheque that buys Alaska for $7.2 million, about two cents an acre. The papers laugh ("Seward's Folly", "Seward's Icebox"). Then you tear it up and the video runs the other world: Russia, broke and unable to defend the land, keeps it; gold (Nome 1898) and oil (Prudhoe Bay 1968) are Russian; the map gets a Russian Alaska touching Canada, a border post, the Cold War seen from the Canadian side; the US is smaller, Texas is the biggest state again, the flag has 49 stars. Button: the placard "Seward's Folly" is turned over and reads "Russia's Folly" (in real life, the folly was Russia's).

## Changed from the brief
- None of the three brief facts changed: $7.2M, 1867, about $0.02/acre, "Seward's Folly" all hold. Added: the exact arithmetic and the second nickname.
- "Two cents an acre" is Wikipedia's rounding. Own calc: 586,412 sq mi x 640 acres = 375,303,680 acres; $7,200,000 / 375,303,680 = $0.0192 per acre, "about two cents" (true, rounds to 2).
- Sources disagree on the Senate vote (37-2 vs 27-12 in one history site); the script does NOT say a vote count.
- "In 1898, three men find gold in a creek" is Nome (Anvil Creek, "Three Lucky Swedes", Sept 1898), not the Klondike (1896, in Canada). Do not draw the Klondike.
- Oil: Prudhoe Bay confirmed 12 Mar 1968, pipeline oil from 1977. Spoken "eighteen billion barrels" is a floor (Alyeska: over 18 billion after 45 years; Wikipedia now says over 19 billion as of 2026), true both ways. The line says "a pipeline moves it", not that all of it came from one field.
- The alt chain (Russia keeps it; gold and oil are Russian; Russian planes in the Cold War) is imagination, said with "would" in the Cold War line and drawn as a story, not a claim. A different alt exists (Russia could have sold to Britain; the sources do not say Britain was offered it). The script picks the simplest one and does not claim it as history.

## True / invented
True: the price and the date; about two cents an acre; "Seward's Folly" / "Icebox"; Russia sold because it was beaten in the Crimean War, thought Alaska hard to defend and feared British forces in Canada; transfer ceremony at Sitka on 18 Oct 1867; gold at Nome 1898; oil at Prudhoe 1968, pipeline since 1977; Alaska is the largest state, more than twice Texas; Alaska was the 49th state (3 Jan 1959).
Invented: you, the cheque in your hands, the crowd, the Officer, the three gold men, the Russian claim fence, the border post, the 49-star flag in this world, Texas's ribbon, the placard flip.
Description draft: "The story is made up; the facts are real. In 1867 the United States bought Alaska from Russia for $7.2 million, about two cents an acre, and critics called it Seward's Folly. Gold was found at Nome in 1898, oil at Prudhoe Bay in 1968, and the pipeline has moved over 18 billion barrels. Alaska is more than twice the size of Texas. Russia, Canada and the Cold War planes are our guess at the other world."
