## Premise
At one moment, all ~8 billion people grind their teeth hard for a while.

## Facts / own calcs
- Bone conduction: your own chewing/grinding reaches the cochlea through the skull, so it is far louder to you than to
  anyone nearby.
- Incoherent sources add by power; spread out, the level at any point is dominated by the nearest sources; high-frequency
  squeaks are absorbed by air (tens of dB per km at ~4 kHz) and blocked by walls → "your family plus the neighbours".
- Max molar bite force in adults ≈ 600-750 N (≈ 70 kgf). 8.1e9 × 70 kg ≈ 5.7e11 kg ≈ 570 Mt; Great Pyramid ≈ 6 Mt
  → ≈ 95 → "a hundred Great Pyramids".
- Internal forces: jaw ↔ skull, net force on Earth ≈ 0.
- Friction heat (own estimate, rounded): μ ≈ 0.15 × 300 N × ~10 mm/s sliding ≈ 0.3-0.5 W per person × 8e9 ≈ 2.5-4 GW
  → "a few big power plants" (≈ 1 GW each). Hedged.
- Enamel: the hardest substance in the human body, harder than bone; acellular after eruption, so it cannot regenerate.
- Sleep bruxism in adults ≈ 12.8 % ± 3.1 (Manfredini et al. 2013, systematic review) → "about one in eight";
  ~5.8e9 adults × 0.128 ≈ 740 M → "hundreds of millions".

## Chain
countdown → grind: gravel in your skull (bone conduction) → neighbour hears a squeak → 8 billion don't make a roar
→ force: 70 kg each, 100 pyramids → Earth feels nothing (internal)
→ real damage: enamel can't regrow → already happening: 1 in 8 adults nightly → night shift → question.
