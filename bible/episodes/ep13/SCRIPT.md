## Premise
You rob a train and ride where no train can go. The railroad answers by using a train to deliver horses.

## Beats
- Paradox: a train chasing you on horses.
- Plant: an old baggage car with a ramp and hay on the floor.
- Mockery: "What's it eat, coal?"
- Count up: one hour, two, five.
- Turn: a second train pulls in and the car side drops open.
- You see: the train was delivering fresh horses. The hay changes meaning.
- Button: you leave the West by train. Loop line: "And that was the last time" + "You robbed a train in Wyoming..."

## True / invented
"You" is an invented outlaw, and the story is a composite of two real robberies. The description must say so.
Draft: "The outlaw in this story is made up, and it combines two real robberies, but the Union Pacific really did build train cars to carry posses and their horses."
