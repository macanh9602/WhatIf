## Premise
Everyone has a second, fully working face (eyes, nose, mouth) on the back of the head. One brain, one body.

## Facts
- Human horizontal visual field ≈ 200° (binocular overlap ≈ 120°), so ≈ 160° behind is unseen. Two faces → ~360°.
- Side sleeping is already the most common sleep position for adults (no number used).
- A large share of the cortex is involved in vision (often quoted 20-30 %, hedged in the script as "a huge part").
- Prey (rabbits): eyes on the sides, almost 360° panoramic view (small blind spots in front/behind).
- Predators (cats, owls, humans): forward-facing eyes, large binocular overlap → stereopsis / depth judgment.
- Payoff is the classic predator-vs-prey eye-placement pattern (a tendency, not a law; hedged as "a clue").

## Chain
second face → 200° + blind spot → full circle → queue stare
→ sleep: side sleepers → brain load ×2 → twist: rabbits see all around (prey) → hunters: eyes in front for depth
→ a back face = prey → your one face = your ancestors chased → question.
