This turkey is about to become a bomb, and you are holding it.
It is rock hard and wearing a little coat of frost, and you hold it over hot oil on Thanksgiving.
Your neighbor leans on the fence.
"That bird is cold."
"It's fine."
So you lower it in, slowly, like a pro.
The oil bubbles. Then it foams. Then it climbs the pot.
Your neighbor steps back, then goes inside and locks the door.
But the oil does not stay in the pot.
It runs over the rim, down the side, and into the flame.
Whoomp.
A fireball rises over the fence, taller than the garage.
You are fine, behind a lawn chair. The lawn chair is not.
You see, that frost was water.
Ice under hot oil turns to steam in a flash, and steam needs seventeen hundred times the room.
The oil has nowhere to go but out, to the flame.
By dusk, a fire engine is parked on your street.
