Russia sold this for two cents an acre.
You stand in Sitka, Alaska, holding the cheque. Seven million dollars.
The newspapers laugh. They say Alaska is nothing but ice. They call it Seward's Folly.
Now tear up the cheque.
America never buys Alaska.
Russia is broke, and it cannot defend the land. British soldiers sit next door, in Canada.
So Russia keeps Alaska, and the flag stays up.
Then, in 1898, three men find gold in a creek. It is Russian gold. You wait outside the fence.
Then comes oil, under the snow. A pipeline moves it. Eighteen billion barrels, and not one is yours.
Look at the new map. Russia now touches Canada, and a border post goes up in the snow.
Later, in the Cold War, Russian planes would fly from here. Canada would be the only thing between them and you.
Alaska is the biggest state, far bigger than Texas. In this world, Texas is the biggest again.
The flag has forty-nine stars.
Seward's Folly? In real life, the folly was Russia's.
