In this town, "I love you" costs three dollars.
You step off the bus in Talkton. Every word here costs ten cents. Even mine.
I am your rented guide. Watch my meter go down.
Mia lives at the end of Main Street. You carry exactly enough for one sentence.
At the market, nobody wastes a word. The baker says, "Bread." You point. Pointing is free.
A man bumps into you. He does not say sorry. Sorry costs five dollars.
You walk on. I keep talking. My meter is half empty, and we have not met Mia.
You cross the old stone bridge. It is long, and old, and pretty.
Why am I describing a bridge? That is money. I will be quiet now.
Night falls. Mia's porch light is on. A neighbor waves from a window. Waving is free.
You climb the steps. You knock. She opens the door.
You say it, and your money jumps into the box. Pling. It is paid in full.
